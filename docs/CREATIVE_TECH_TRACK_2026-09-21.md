# OMAR CREATIVE TECHNOLOGY TRACK
Date: 2026-09-21
Status: ACTIVE
Anchor project: FIRST CONTACT / Creative Technology Lab

## Declaration

The next phase is not "learn more frontend."

The goal is to become capable of designing and building experiences where software, image, sound, bodies, devices, physical objects, spaces, data and networks behave as one medium.

The browser is not treated as a page renderer. It is treated as an accessible runtime for interactive worlds.

Omar is deliberately tool-open. No framework, language, engine, vendor or medium is sacred. Tools are selected by the experience, not the other way around.

## Working principle

We do not finish a course and then make art.

We build real experiments. Each experiment forces one or more new technical capabilities into the toolbox. Every capability should become visible, testable and reusable.

Learning loop:

1. Define the human experience.
2. Identify the physical/digital signals involved.
3. Prototype the smallest real interaction.
4. Observe it on a real device / in a real space.
5. Refine the sensory behavior.
6. Extract reusable technical primitives.
7. Archive what was learned.
8. Combine it with the next medium.

## Experience laws

- No technology is included just to demonstrate technology.
- The body/device/object/space should feel like part of the piece, not a controller bolted onto it.
- Reduce explicit UI whenever discovery can teach the interaction.
- Permission prompts are unavoidable system boundaries; after permission, the experience should become as direct as possible.
- Sound, light, motion and material state are first-class interface elements.
- Real-world consequences matter more than screen spectacle.
- Build accessibility and fallbacks rather than assuming one perfect device.
- Camera/microphone interactions must be explicit and permission-based.
- Prefer on-device processing when raw camera/audio does not need to leave the device.
- Every experiment must survive a real-device test before being called complete.

## Experiment family

### BODY://01 — NO BUTTONS
Camera -> movement field -> shader matter -> reactive stereo sound.
Purpose: prove that the body can control a web world without conventional UI.

### DEVICE://01 — TILT IS INPUT
Gyroscope / accelerometer -> 3D world / sound / haptics.
Purpose: make the phone itself become the interface.

### VISION://01 — THE WORLD NOTICES
Pose / hand / gesture tracking -> scene logic.
Purpose: move from raw motion to semantic bodily gestures.

### SIGNAL://01 — TWO PRESENCES
Realtime two-person world.
Purpose: one person's movement becomes material in another person's browser.

### SPACE://01 — SCREEN ESCAPES
Projection + tracked movement.
Purpose: move the browser experience from a rectangle into a room.

### OBJECT://02 — PHYSICAL STATE
NFC + sensor/controller + web state.
Purpose: a physical object both opens and changes its digital world.

### XR://01 — PLACE IT HERE
WebXR / AR.
Purpose: bind digital matter to physical space.

The sequence is not a prison. Experiments can merge, split or reorder when a stronger idea demands it.

## Definition of mastery

Mastery is not knowing every API.

Mastery means being able to imagine an interaction, identify the required signals and mediums, choose an appropriate technical stack, build it, tune the sensory result, test it in reality, and combine it with other mediums without being trapped by one tool.

The long-term target is a body of work that is difficult to classify as only website, app, game, artwork, product or installation.

It is an experience system.

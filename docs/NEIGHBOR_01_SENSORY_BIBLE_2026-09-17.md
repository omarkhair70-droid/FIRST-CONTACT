# NEIGHBOR_01 — Sensory Bible

Status: creative north star for PR #4. This document defines the recipient experience before backend wiring. The lab is allowed to exaggerate transitions; production should keep the same emotional logic with less visible tooling.

## Core thesis

NEIGHBOR_01 is not a portfolio, dating gimmick, or 3D showcase. It is a designed first encounter between two people who are physically near but socially unresolved.

The work does not try to manufacture chemistry or persuade the recipient. It changes the starting conditions: it makes room for reinterpretation, creates one shared event, gives the recipient agency, and deliberately leaves the final part to real life.

North star: **the world begins as stone and ends as if it has started to live.**

Emotional curve: **safe strangeness → curiosity → misalignment → reinterpretation → signal → shared life → reveal → voluntary reciprocity → incomplete warmth → real-world remainder.**

## Material language

Cold phase: chalk, plaster, fog, graphite, dry porcelain, low saturation, large negative space.

Transition phase: dusty rose, clay, peach, translucent porcelain membranes, softer shadows, slightly closer camera.

Living phase: coral, amber, restrained burgundy, internal light, organic motion. Warm color is an event and must stay scarce.

Never use literal red hearts, Valentine's graphics, rose emojis, cyberpunk neon, hacker UI, startup dashboards, or a generic Awwwards gradient. A flower may exist only as an abstract porcelain organism born from the space between the two nodes.

## Sonic language

No song and no conventional soundtrack. Sound behaves like a material in the room.

- Object: near-silence, air, low ceramic resonance.
- Gap: dry mono room tone; one distant pulse.
- Angle: a restrained scrape/tension interval that settles when alignment resolves.
- Signal: isolated glass-like pulses; transmissions can stall before reaching the second node.
- Third Thing: first breathy harmonic layer. The world feels alive for the first time.
- Reveal: brief silence before Omar is named.
- Name: a second stable tone joins the first; two presences now exist.
- 91%: an almost-resolving chord deliberately missing its final note.
- Unchanged: warmth and harmonics recede without a sad sting.

Sound begins only after the recipient's first intentional touch because browsers require a user gesture and because the gesture should feel like waking the object.

## Haptics

Haptics are rare punctuation, not button feedback.

- Wake: one short ceramic-like tick.
- Alignment: two soft clicks.
- Signal arrival: a quick travelling pulse.
- Name accepted: one longer soft pulse.
- 91% stop: pulse, pause, smaller pulse, then nothing.
- Unchanged: no dramatic vibration.

## Scene Bible

### 00 — OBJECT 001 / 0–6s

What is seen: a small off-white porcelain/plaster artifact floating in a quiet field. It feels physical before it feels digital. No romantic color yet.

Copy should be minimal: `OBJECT 001` and a tiny invitation such as `touch to wake`.

What happens on touch: the soundscape wakes, the artifact gives one haptic tick, and the camera moves through the object rather than loading a new page.

Meaning: this is an object carrying an encounter, not a website link.

### 01 — THE GAP / 6–14s

What is seen: two architectural masses/nodes are visibly close but separated by a narrow negative space. Physical distance looks trivial; social distance feels unresolved.

Copy: `SMALL DISTANCE / UNKNOWN MEANING`.

No explanation of Omar or Yasmeen.

Meaning: proximity does not equal connection.

### 02 — ANGLE / 14–25s

What is seen: two sculptural fragments initially read as broken, opposing, or awkward. As the viewpoint shifts, they align into one calm composition. The pieces themselves do not change identity; only their relation changes.

Copy: `THE THING DIDN'T CHANGE. THE ANGLE DID.`

Haptic: soft double click at alignment.

Meaning: reinterpretation. Misalignment is not evidence of rejection, hostility, or intent.

### 03 — SIGNAL / 25–35s

What is seen: a light packet leaves one node. One attempt stalls, one fades, one finally crosses when the recipient touches/holds at the right moment. It must not feel like a skill game.

Copy: `A SIGNAL CAN EXIST WITHOUT ARRIVING.`

Meaning: silence/absence is ambiguous; transmission and reception are different events.

### 04 — THIRD THING / 35–50s

What is seen: neither node moves closer. Instead, a new form grows in the negative space between them. It is a porcelain membrane bloom: between a flower, folded paper, a marine organism, and a small sculpture. It has a warm internal core.

Interaction: touching/holding a petal causes warm light to travel through it like a vein. It should react locally to the touch, not spin as a whole model.

Copy can disappear almost entirely here. This scene should be understood by sight and sound.

Meaning: person → shared object → person. A third thing carries the pressure of the first encounter so neither person has to carry it alone.

### 05 — REVEAL / 50–58s

Before text: cut the sound for roughly half a second.

The bloom remains alive.

Reveal copy:

`أنا عمر، جارك.`

Then, smaller/self-aware:

`وكان ممكن أقول هاي عادي. واضح إن الموضوع خرج عن السيطرة شوية.`

Then the serious sentence:

`دي بس فرصة إن المرة الجاية ما تبدأش من صفر.`

Meaning: humor releases pressure. The reveal recontextualizes everything without turning into a portfolio.

### 06 — SECOND NODE / 58–70s

The second node is explicitly voluntary. In the real recipient flow, the user may enter any name/nickname or remain unknown. In the art-direction lab we use `YASMEEN` to evaluate the composition.

When a name enters:

- the `?` node gains a halo;
- the name is integrated into the spatial composition rather than only shown inside an input;
- the shared bloom opens one additional membrane;
- a second stable sonic tone appears;
- warmth increases but does not become red/romantic.

Critical rule: this must be the first major visual change caused by the recipient, not by Omar's biography.

### 07 — AGENCY / 70–82s

Three outcomes, all emotionally valid:

1. `91% / leave the rest to real life`
2. `SIGNAL / send one thing through`
3. `UNCHANGED / close cleanly`

Do not label one as the preferred or brave option. Do not punish exit with sad music, decay, red error states, or guilt copy.

### 08A — 91% / 82–94s

What is seen: a circular/bridging structure assembles toward completion and stops at 91%. The missing segment remains visible and physically possible, but is not filled digitally.

Sound: almost-resolved chord, final note absent.

Haptic: pulse → pause → smaller pulse → silence.

Copy:

`91%`

`THE REST DOESN'T BELONG TO THIS SCREEN.`

`NEXT ENCOUNTER / IRL`

Meaning: the web refuses to simulate the real encounter.

### 08B — SIGNAL / 82–96s

The typed sentence becomes a small luminous particle/strand that literally travels across the gap. After arrival, completion may rise to 96%, never 100%.

Copy: `A LITTLE MORE CROSSED. NOT EVERYTHING.`

Meaning: digital communication can move the state forward without replacing real life.

### 08C — UNCHANGED / 82–90s

Warm color slowly recedes toward chalk/bone. The bloom folds but does not die, shatter, wilt, or become tragic.

Copy: `NOTHING CHANGED. THAT'S ALLOWED.`

No follow-up is owed.

## Yasmeen composition note

The recipient's known name in the current art-direction exercise is YASMEEN. Do not use this to imply prior surveillance or hidden knowledge in the production encounter. In the real flow, a name must only enter after the recipient voluntarily supplies it. The lab may use `YASMEEN` as a controlled visual placeholder for typography, scale, sound, and bloom response.

The fact that the recipient studies Fine Arts is a creative quality bar, not a profiling mechanism. The work should withstand an art student's eye by having intentional composition, material logic, negative space, rhythm, and restraint. It should not pander with generic art-school symbols.

## Interaction laws

1. Every movement must reveal meaning, increase curiosity, or advance the relationship state.
2. No effect exists only because the 3D stack can do it.
3. The recipient must never have to solve a difficult puzzle to earn the reveal.
4. The experience should create a shared reference, not a debt.
5. Warmth appears because something changed between nodes, not because Omar is trying to signal romance.
6. Humor enters at reveal to prevent the artifact becoming self-serious or emotionally coercive.
7. The most important consequence should happen after the phone closes.

## Production gate

Do not merge the creative branch into `main` until:

- recipient preview can be played end-to-end without backend;
- lab tooling can be hidden so the work reads as an encounter, not a debug page;
- mobile composition is visually reviewed on a real narrow viewport;
- sound can be enabled with one intentional touch and never autoplays before consent;
- reduced-motion still preserves meaning;
- the real flow never reveals a recipient name before voluntary entry;
- UNCHANGED is as polished as 91%;
- no scene reads as a portfolio or technology demo.

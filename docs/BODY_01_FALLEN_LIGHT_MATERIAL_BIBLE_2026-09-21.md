# BODY://01 MATERIAL IDENTITY — FALLEN LIGHT
Date: 2026-09-21
Status: ACTIVE MATERIAL DIRECTION

## Core idea

FALLEN LIGHT is not an angel model and not a demon model.

It is one living material with a memory of how the body has treated it.

The body does not puppeteer the object.
The body reveals what the object is becoming.

## State language

The material has continuous internal dimensions rather than costume swaps:

- energy — what is happening now
- memory — what recent movement has left behind
- corruption — accumulated instability / fall
- calm — sustained stillness / recovery pressure
- position — where movement is happening

These values feed the same form, shader and sound system.

## Visual arc

### Luminous
- pearl / chalk body
- soft internal gold
- slow breathing
- rounded deformation
- clean silhouette

### Remembering
- motion leaves warm veins
- old movement remains visible after the body stops
- the object starts to feel less neutral

### Unstable
- asymmetry grows
- the surface develops darker paths
- deformation becomes sharper
- sound detunes gradually

### Fallen
- wine / ash enters the surface
- procedural cracks become visible
- sharper fields appear in geometry
- the form leans and compresses instead of simply pulsing
- harmony becomes unstable rather than switching to a cartoon "evil" cue

### Re-forming
- stillness reduces corruption
- light returns gradually
- scars can remain longer than immediate energy
- recovery is a transformation, not a reset button

## Interaction law

Movement should not equal animation one-to-one.

Movement feeds a material system with inertia and memory.

Fast motion can push the system toward instability.
Stillness can recover it.
Repeated behavior changes how the next movement feels.

## Why this direction

The original "angel -> demon" instinct is preserved as:
grace -> instability -> fall -> another form

But the symbolism lives in:
- silhouette
- material
- light
- sound
- temporal behavior

not literal wings, horns or character cosplay.

## Current implementation — v2

BODY://01 currently contains:
- local camera motion analysis
- motion centroid
- motion energy
- accumulated material memory
- corruption/recovery dynamics
- shader-driven veins/cracks
- corruption-driven asymmetric displacement
- pearl/gold -> coral/wine/ash color transition
- procedural Web Audio detuning and filtering
- stereo position mapped from movement

No raw camera frames are uploaded by this slice.

## Next material evolution

The next major step is semantic body understanding.

Raw motion remains useful, but VISION://01 will add:
- hand openness
- wrist positions
- shoulder angle
- head position
- body distance
- two-arm gestures
- sustained pose states

Then material transitions can respond to meaning rather than motion amount alone.

Examples:
- open hand -> bloom / expose inner light
- closed posture -> defense / compression
- arms raised -> ascent state
- asymmetrical pose -> controlled fracture
- long stillness -> re-form
- approach camera -> confrontation / depth response

## Rule

Do not add visual complexity unless the body can discover a relationship with it.

The target is not "cool shader."
The target is a material that feels as if it has a nervous system.
